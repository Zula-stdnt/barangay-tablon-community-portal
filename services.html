Barangay Services & Contact

We are here to serve you. Find out how we can help and how to reach us.

Our Services

Programs and assistance provided by the Barangay Local Government Unit

📄

Barangay Clearance & Certificates

Issuance of barangay clearances, certificate of residency, indigency, and other essential local government documents required for employment, business, and school.

🤝

Community Programs

Support initiatives including drug rehabilitation assistance, livelihood training, and various social welfare programs targeted at empowering residents.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

People's Day Assistance

Special events offering free medical check-ups, rice distribution, scholarship assistance, job application help, and late birth registration.

📶

Public Wi-Fi & Digital Access

Free public Wi-Fi access in designated areas to help students and residents with online research, communications, and digital transactions.

Resident Feedback & Inquiries

Have a question or community concern? Submit an inquiry directly to the Barangay Desk.

Contact & Office Hours

Visit the Barangay Hall or reach out to us via phone or social media for inquiries and assistance.
