Tablon National High School

Healthcare Facilities

🏥
Primary Care Facility

CDO City Hospital – Tablon Primary Care

Located at Purok 4, Umalag St., this PhilHealth-accredited facility serves as an extension of the CDO City Hospital to provide accessible healthcare to eastern barangays.

Available Services:
  • Outpatient consultations
  • Animal Bite Treatment Center
  • Pharmacy, Laboratory, and Diagnostics
  • Minor surgery and Family Planning

📞 Contact: 0927-424-8752
